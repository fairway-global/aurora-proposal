# Reviewer Brief

The AURORA Treasury Proposal requests **940,000 ADA** to establish shared open-source infrastructure for institutional credit markets on Cardano over approximately **five months** and **four implementation milestones**.

**Development status:** AURORA is ready to begin the proposed development. Treasury-funded development has not started. It will begin following approval and enactment of the Treasury Withdrawal governance action and receipt by Fairway of the initial implementation instalment.

## What Treasury Funds

Treasury funding supports the Metadata Standard, Verification Framework, AURORA Discovery Engine, open APIs and filtering capabilities, Capital Provider Profile Standard and Reference Query Library, developer tooling and reference implementation, technical documentation, and independent security and legal review. Delivery culminates in an end-to-end testnet demonstration and public open-source release. Independent Treasury-use audit and oversight are included in the governance arrangements.

The initial allocation comprises **800,000 ADA** as a fixed implementation fee payable to Fairway and **140,000 ADA** for Review, Hosting & Technical Contingency. The fee is assessed against agreed outputs, not reimbursed against internal costs. Administrators may approve reallocations, including additional implementation payments, within the approved scope and total budget while preserving required reviews, audits and existing commitments. At the proposal's illustrative reference price of **US$0.20 per ADA**, the total is approximately **US$188,000**. AURORA provides shared market infrastructure; it does not originate loans, custody lending capital, or make capital-allocation decisions. Funding and settlement remain with the underlying lending infrastructure.

## Delivery and Governance

Fairway is lead implementer and project coordinator. Sundial contributes selected technical input, and Fallen Icarus (Rusty) serves as technical advisor and reviewer. If the Treasury Withdrawal is approved and enacted, the full allocation will initially be received into a dedicated **3-of-5 AURORA Treasury multisignature**. The proposed administrators are James "Blockjock" Meidinger, Christian Taylor, Elder Millennial, Wilco USDM, and Eddie D, all independent of the implementation participants.

Milestone allocations are **140,000 / 320,000 / 290,000 / 190,000 ADA** for M1 through M4. These are funding envelopes for implementation and supporting costs, not automatically Fairway's individual payments. Implementation instalments are paid in advance to Fairway's designated wallet. The cumulative disbursement limit is **140,000 ADA** at commencement, **460,000 ADA** after M1 acceptance, **750,000 ADA** after M2 acceptance and **940,000 ADA** after M3 acceptance.

Fairway controls its implementation wallet, staffing, subcontracting and internal resource allocation. Routine implementation expenses do not require administrator approval. Administrators review milestone evidence, authorize disbursements and may suspend further payments for material delivery problems or fund misuse.

An independent Treasury-use audit follows publication of M2 technical outputs, separately from M2 technical acceptance. The final audit is completed during M4 before closure. Section 11 defines the audit scope: administrator balances, authorized payments, supporting costs, reallocations, delivery and material findings, rather than routine reconciliation of Fairway's internal costs. Funds remaining under administrator custody are returned if the project terminates under Section 9; an advance does not constitute acceptance of unfinished work or remove Fairway's delivery responsibility.
