# AURORA

Open Infrastructure for Institutional Credit Markets on Cardano

AURORA requests **940,000 ADA** from the Cardano Treasury to establish shared open-source market infrastructure for Cardano credit markets, supporting discovery, filtering, verification and evaluation across compatible lending implementations. Delivery is planned over approximately **five months** through **four implementation milestones**.

**Development status:** AURORA is ready to begin the proposed development. Treasury-funded development has not started. It will begin following approval and enactment of the Treasury Withdrawal governance action and receipt by Fairway of the initial implementation instalment.

The Treasury-funded implementation focuses on building and validating the shared technical infrastructure described in the proposal. The initial allocation comprises **800,000 ADA** as a fixed implementation fee payable to Fairway and **140,000 ADA** for Review, Hosting & Technical Contingency. Administrator-approved reallocations may adjust these allocations within the approved scope and total request, while preserving funding for required reviews, audits and existing commitments.

## What Treasury Funds

The funded public outputs include the **Metadata Standard, Verification Framework, AURORA Discovery Engine, open APIs and filtering capabilities, Capital Provider Profile Standard and Reference Query Library, developer tooling and reference implementation, technical documentation, and independent security and legal review**. Delivery culminates in an end-to-end testnet demonstration and public open-source release. Their implementation home is the [AURORA implementation repository](https://github.com/fairway-global/cardano-aurora); this repository maintains the Treasury proposal and its translations.

AURORA does not replace lending protocols, originate loans, custody lending capital, or decide which opportunities receive funding. Funding and settlement remain functions of the compatible lending infrastructure underlying each opportunity.

## Governance Positioning

If the Treasury Withdrawal is approved and enacted, the full allocation will initially be received into a dedicated **3-of-5 AURORA Treasury multisignature**, controlled by independent administrators. Implementation instalments are paid in advance to Fairway's designated implementation wallet, beginning at project commencement; subsequent releases follow acceptance of the preceding milestone. The cumulative disbursement limits are **140,000 / 460,000 / 750,000 / 940,000 ADA** and cover implementation payments and supporting costs, not Fairway's individual payments alone.

Fairway controls its implementation wallet and internal delivery resources. Routine implementation expenses do not require administrator approval. Independent Treasury-use audits follow publication of the M2 technical outputs and take place during M4 before closure. The interim audit is separate from M2 technical acceptance; the audit scope is defined in Section 11. Administrators may suspend further disbursements for material delivery problems or fund misuse. Funds remaining under administrator custody are subject to the refund provisions in Section 9.
